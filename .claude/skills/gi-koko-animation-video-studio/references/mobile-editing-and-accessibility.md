# Mobile-first editing, accessibility and delivery

- Design for small phone screens first: large type, strong contrast, clear hierarchy and uncluttered compositions.
- Keep titles, subtitles, logos and credits within platform safe areas.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning. Use readable contrast and captions for speech.
- Avoid flashing patterns and rapid strobing. Use smooth motion and allow enough reading time.
- Reframe landscape footage intelligently for 9:16; track the subject rather than applying a blind center crop.
- Keep exports compatible with modest Android devices. Offer 480p/720p fallback and avoid huge bitrates when the user targets an entry-level phone.
- Preserve originals and provide editable project files when feasible.
- Package final MP4, thumbnail, English SRT, Myanmar SRT, narration/script, source ledger and README.
